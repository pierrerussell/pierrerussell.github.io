Project Overview

The Problem

Microsoft Teams displays real-time presence status (Available, Busy, Away) but deliberately does NOT store historical data for privacy reasons. Organizations managing remote and hybrid teams have no way to answer:

  • When were employees actually online yesterday?
  • What are our team's typical working hours?
  • Who is working excessive out-of-hours time?
  • Is there a pattern of availability issues?

The Solution

The Green Dots bridges this gap by continuously capturing presence status and providing:

  • Hour-by-hour presence timelines for assigned team members
  • Automated daily/weekly/monthly email reports with analytics
  • Real-time dashboard with availability insights
  • Working hours analysis and out-of-hours detection
  • Per-seat subscription billing with 7-day free trial
